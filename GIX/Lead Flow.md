# GIX Lead Flow Tracking with Actions

Terms:
| Name | Description|
| --- | --- |
| Lead | Name/Contact information from an interested persion with not additional information. |
| Prospect | Lead that expresses additional interest though interaction with MSTI program through appointments, events, etc. |
| Qualified | Prospect or Lead that has clear indications that they are a good fit or strongly interested in the program, including started but not submitted application |
| Applicant | Someone who has submitted an application for the MSTI or Dual Degree Program. |
| Admit | Applicant that has been offered admission |
| Accept | Applicant that has accepted an admissions offer |
| Deposited | Applicant that has paid their ECD |
| Defer | Deposited but deferred admission (includes DD} |
| Deny | Denied Admission
| Withdrawn | Withdrawn or declined admission offer |
